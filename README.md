# llms.txt

The `llms.txt` initiative is an open, community-driven effort that empowers website owners to declare their informational structure to Large Language Models (LLMs), similar to how `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` govern access by search engine crawlers.

## What is llms.txt?

`llms.txt` is a plaintext file placed at the root of your domain (e.g., `https://example.com/llms.txt`). It describes the website pages that should be used for training or any other purpose; LLM may use it for.

This approach promotes transparency and respect for content rights in the age of AI-powered tools and platforms.

## Why Use llms.txt?

- **Transparency** – Make your content usage policy clear to AI crawlers.
- **Standardization** – Join a growing number of publishers in adopting a common communication method with LLMs.
